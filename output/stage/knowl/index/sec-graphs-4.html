With any type of two-dimensional display of data, the most important thing is to remember the underlying grid; especially if it is only underlying and not visible. The grid has horizontal lines (for a graph, or rows in chart) and vertical lines (called caolumns in a chart). Horizontal elements are side-to-side (like the horizon) and vertical elements are up and down. Table tops, floors, and ceilings are all horizontal, and walls and doors are vertical.
