The frequency column represents a count of how many times a particular response appears. This should always be a whole number. Both of the relative frequency columns put that count into the context of the whole. It is a good idea to have both in your table. You can revisit Section 3.1 to review the details of the percentage formula and the pros and cons of both absolute and relative change. The total of the frequency column should always be the sample size. The relative frequency column should total to 1 or 100%, but can sometimes be a little bit off because of rounding.
